challenge: "In a category where most KYC and Fraud Prevention brands look alike, Sumsub iGaming needed a distinctive visual identity. At the same time, the project already had a recognizable asset: 3D cubes had been used across campaigns, reports, and events, but more as standalone illustrations than as part of a cohesive system. The challenge was to preserve that recognition, transform it into a scalable identity, and prove that security can look just as fast, bold, and exciting as the iGaming industry itself."
solution: "We preserved the brand's strongest visual asset and turned it into the foundation of the entire identity. A single 3D cube became the core key visual, evolving with each context through changes in content, surrounding environment, color accents, and composition while maintaining a recognizable structure. Around it, we built a flexible design system of tech-inspired gradients, expressive typography, product interfaces, and dynamic layouts. We also added a bold, futuristic, colorful photo style, fully AI-generated—quick concepting via Midjourney prompts and moodboards, refined for realism with Enhancor when needed. The result is a brand that remains consistent while offering virtually limitless creative flexibility."
