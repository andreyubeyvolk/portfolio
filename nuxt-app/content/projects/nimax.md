challenge: "In the digital agency category, visual language is part of reputation. It has to read as current to clients who live in tech aesthetics themselves. Nimax's system hadn't been updated in over five years. It was dark, near-black, serious and premium, with 3D illustrations as its signature move. The task was to refresh and modernize the language without losing the recognition and weight built up over the years."
solution: "The core decision was to refresh Nimax's dark theme—opening it up toward a lighter, more technological future, and updating the color palette. Nimax's own groundwork became the foundation, including their base of 3D illustrations, the signature move carried into the new vibe. The dark base gave way to a light one, with vivid mint as the primary color and purple as a secondary accent, paired with the TT Hoves typeface. Aggressive caps were dropped from headlines, graphic tags were simplified, and large rounded shapes softened the system—while sharpness stayed where it holds the character: typography, dividing lines, type shifts. The system made it to physical objects too, with several merch items, including tote bags, actually shipping. Banner carousels now feature banners the Nimax team built on their own, without me, but within the style that was set—proof of how it kept living on. Sparking this change feels meaningful: the brand is now lighter, more modern, and more digital, with a foundation for its continued evolution."
